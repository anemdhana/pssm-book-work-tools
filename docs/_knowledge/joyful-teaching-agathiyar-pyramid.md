**Brahmarshi Patriji’s Simple Yet Profound Message on Eyes, Breath, and Meditation**

### A Warm Welcome Among Friends

At the Agathiyar Pyramid Dhyana Ashram in Tamil Nadu, the atmosphere is one of natural joy and openness. Brahmarshi Patriji, the founder of the Pyramid Spiritual Societies Movement, greets everyone with his characteristic warmth and playful spirit.

“Namaste, good morning. How is the pyramid? How is our boss? Can you give me a kiss?” he asks with a smile. In that light-hearted moment, he instantly dissolves formality. He is not a distant guru on a pedestal, but a friend who walks alongside seekers.

### The Two Keys to Enlightenment

Patriji then turns to the heart of his teaching with remarkable clarity:

> “Two things everybody has to understand — one about the eyes, the other about the breath. If you understood these two things, you would have become enlightened.”

He explains that everyone who comes to the pyramid has come precisely for this understanding — to learn about their eyes and their breath.

### Conserving and Maximizing Energy

With simple, practical wisdom, he continues:

> “You conserve your energy by closing your eyes and closing your mouth, and you maximize your energy when you are with your breath. Meditation means conserving your body’s energy and increasing your Soul’s energy.”

In just a few sentences, he captures the essence of Anapanasati (breath awareness) meditation as practiced in the PSSM tradition:

- **Closing the eyes and mouth** reduces the constant outflow of energy through the senses and unnecessary speech.
- **Staying with the natural breath** gathers and multiplies inner energy.
- The result is a shift: the body becomes calm and conserved, while the soul’s energy expands.

### A Message of Joy and Inspiration

Patriji’s words are filled with genuine delight at seeing the gathering:

> “I’m so very happy to see you all. It’s always such a joy to see you… and such a great work you are doing. You are a great inspiration to all the whole of the world.”

He does not speak from a place of authority alone, but from shared joy. The seekers at Agathiyar Pyramid are not just students; they are fellow travelers whose dedication itself becomes a light for others.

### The Essence in Brief

At the Agathiyar Pyramid Dhyana Ashram, Patriji reminds everyone that enlightenment is not complicated. It rests on two simple understandings:

1. **The eyes** — close them to conserve energy.
2. **The breath** — stay with it to maximize the energy of the soul.

Meditation, in this view, is the art of conserving the body’s energy while expanding the soul’s. Surrounded by the quiet power of the pyramids and the natural beauty of the ashram, this teaching is offered with the warmth of a friend and the clarity of a realized master.

This short interaction captures the spirit of Agathiyar Pyramid Dhyana Ashram: a place where silence, breath, and joyful presence come together under the loving guidance of Brahmarshi Patriji.
